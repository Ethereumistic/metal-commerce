## Generated MORIS product imagery — 2026-10-06

The Trestle, Round and Low designs were generated with OpenAI ImageGen for MORIS FURNITURE, using each white studio master as the reference for its five other views. These are generated design visualizations, not photographs of manufactured samples. All views depict the Natural matte oil finish; merchant descriptions and FAQs explain reclaimed grain variation and the Smoked finish.

Original PNGs are retained in `artifacts/moris-products/{trestle,round,low}/01.png` through `06.png`. Commerce stores the uploaded full-size lossless WebP media and supplies the live URLs. Ordering is studio, interior, top detail, support detail, interior, interior. Product galleries do not load these local originals.
