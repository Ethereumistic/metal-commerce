# Metal Craft — 24 premade product concepts

Planning concepts · revised 10 October 2026 with measured product scale and machine capacity

Start with the [inventory direction](README.md). The [visual sketches](visual-direction.md) explain the intended appearance; the [rights guide](rights-and-custom-boundaries.md) applies to every design.

**Each proposed catalog design needs original artwork or documented rights.** The [owner's maternity-gift reference](production-scale-reference.md) is a real workshop product, measured by the owner at **300 mm wide × 600 mm high**, approximately **2–2.5 mm thick**. Proposed variants are not automatically ready or rights-cleared. Names are working titles, not cleared trademarks. Other dimensions are proposed outer dimensions in millimeters, before sample validation; round dimensions are diameters. They are not measurements taken from the shirt, and they do not define the machine's minimum size. A set's dimensions refer to each part unless stated otherwise.

The core is substantial silhouettes, metal pictures, and large gifts. The earlier medallion, bookmark, and miniature-ornament concepts remain outside the core for commercial reasons. Technical staff reportedly confirmed small detailed cuts are possible; the earlier shuriken/kunai comparison is not a machine minimum. Compact functional objects are secondary conditional development. See the [machine specifications](../laser-machine-specs.md) for current capacity and the expected December 2026 upgrade.

## Zodiac and celestial

**Superseding direction from the owner:** use a square or circular outline with a 1:1 bounding box, the exact centered `TbZodiac[Sign]` icon from `react-icons/tb`, and the corresponding supplied constellation SVG. The natural animal silhouettes and separate glyph-only concepts below are retained as earlier planning history, not the active zodiac specification. The first twelve product visualizations will use square plaques; see the [active imagery brief](../../artifacts/zodiac-products/BRIEF.md).

### MC-ZOD-01 — Zodiac animal and symbol silhouettes · P0

**Imagine:** a large original lion for Leo, with a bold mane, readable facial openings, and a natural outer silhouette. Each zodiac sign becomes its own substantial subject: an animal, paired figures, scales, or another traditional motif. The subject determines the outline; there is no default medallion or badge frame.

- **Prototype:** proposed Leo envelope 650 × 450; possible later 850 × 590 after a separately reviewed drawing. First finish: matte black. Mounting positions belong in substantial retained areas.
- **Future designs:** ram, bull, paired figures, crab, lion, original maiden figure, scales, scorpion, original archer, goat, water bearer, and paired fish for the twelve signs. All drawn independently, without copying an astrology poster or icon pack.
- **Construction:** mane, horns, limbs, arrows, and other details need broad connected structure and validated openings. Each sign gets its own geometry; keep exposed tips intentional and avoid tiny decorative perforations.
- **Buyer/display:** birthdays, personal spaces, gifts; hang a pair for a couple or a trio for a family.
- **Release condition:** test Leo first, then validate all other drawings separately. Names or sign labels are optional later layouts with separately validated lettering.

### MC-ZOD-02 — Bold zodiac glyph · P1

**Imagine:** a large traditional zodiac glyph made from broad connected metal bands, with the symbol itself defining the outline. The piece has the scale of wall artwork, with no miniature tile or medallion treatment.

- **Targets:** proposed approximately 500 × 600 envelope, adapted to each glyph's proportions; matte black or warm white after finish approval.
- **Designs:** the same twelve sign choices as MC-ZOD-01, redrawn in our own geometric grammar. Do not copy a proprietary font's outlines without checking its license.
- **Construction:** broad bands and deliberate bridges where needed. Mounting locations are integrated into thick retained areas, with a backing/assembly considered only if a glyph cannot be made as one stable object.
- **Buyer/display:** a graphic large zodiac alternative; one statement symbol or a substantial paired arrangement.
- **Release condition:** the glyph reads correctly after bridges are added, and each size has sufficient retained metal.

## Automotive and motion

These are **invented vehicles**. A missing badge does not make a traced branded car our original design. Compare the final drawing for recognizable borrowed bodywork before approval; see the [rights guide](rights-and-custom-boundaries.md#automotive-designs).

### MC-AUTO-01 — Canyon Coupe · P0

**Imagine:** a low, wide two-door coupe in side profile, with a flowing roof and chunky wheel forms attached to a continuous lower rail. Large window cutouts provide contrast. No badge, make, model name, distinctive copied grille, or race livery.

- **Targets:** proposed 800 × 300, later 1,000 × 375 with a separately approved layout; matte black.
- **Construction:** broad roof, sill, and wheel connections. Tires stay attached; wheel openings use a stencil arrangement. At least two spaced mounting points designed into retained metal.
- **Buyer/display:** garage, office, automotive enthusiast gift. A horizontal anchor above a workbench or shelf.
- **Release condition:** long sections stay flat after cutting/coating; the packaging supports the silhouette and wheel areas.

### MC-AUTO-02 — Open Roadster · P1

**Imagine:** an invented open-top car with a long curved hood, small cabin, and simplified rounded fenders. An expressive automotive drawing rather than an exact real-world replica.

- **Targets:** proposed 800 × 340, later 1,000 × 425; matte black; muted bronze-color coating as a later option.
- **Construction:** merge the windshield support into a thicker cabin shape. Avoid fragile free-standing windshield lines and isolated circular wheel centers.
- **Buyer/display:** a softer automotive piece that can sit comfortably in a living room.
- **Release condition:** independent sketch and comparison review; windshield and fender details survive sample handling.

### MC-AUTO-03 — Apex, top view · P1

**Imagine:** a symmetrical fictional race-inspired vehicle viewed from above, arranged inside a narrow rectangular border. Wide wheel shapes, a tapered cabin, and restrained open channels suggest movement.

- **Targets:** proposed 350 × 800, later 440 × 1,000; matte black.
- **Construction:** frame-connected body and wheels. Every opening is a cutout, not an implied engraved line. Keep the design free of team names, sponsors, and real-car aerodynamic signatures.
- **Buyer/display:** vertical wall space; complementary to the side-profile cars.
- **Release condition:** the view remains legible without an engraved layer; independently review the drawing's source and appearance.

## Nature and landscape

### MC-NAT-01 — Ridge at Dawn · P0

**Imagine:** a rectangular metal picture with three broad mountain ridges, a rising sun, and open sky. The sun is supported by the ridge or by planned bridges; its center is never a loose disk.

- **Targets:** proposed 650 × 400, later 850 × 520; matte black. Slight wall offset may create a second landscape in shadow if the mounting sample supports it.
- **Construction:** common outer frame connecting the scene; substantial ridge bands. No unsupported tiny peaks.
- **Buyer/display:** living rooms and offices; an accessible first statement piece.
- **Release condition:** the sun and ridges remain attached, frame stays flat, and mounting positions are balanced.

### MC-NAT-02 — Olive Branch · P0

**Imagine:** an asymmetric branch sweeping diagonally, with broad rounded leaves and a few open leaf centers. The structure feels calm and airy, with much of the wall visible.

- **Targets:** proposed 650 × 350, later 850 × 460; matte black, then warm white.
- **Construction:** one substantial central stem; leaves join through broad necks. Mounting tabs concealed within larger leaf/stem areas. Round exposed tips in the geometry and finish the edges.
- **Buyer/display:** above a console, beside a mirror, or as a softer bedroom piece.
- **Release condition:** no easily bent leaf tips, and the asymmetric shape hangs level with the actual hardware.

### MC-NAT-03 — Forest Window · P1

**Imagine:** a tall open frame containing three stylized trees, with solid trunks and simplified canopy cutouts. Tree bases merge into a common ground band.

- **Targets:** proposed 400 × 800, later 500 × 1,000; matte black.
- **Construction:** trunks connect to the ground and selected branches to the side frame. Use a few large canopy openings instead of hundreds of small leaf holes.
- **Buyer/display:** narrow walls, hallways, cabins, nature gifts.
- **Release condition:** the frame and trees form a robust retained network; cutting time stays reasonable for the actual nesting plan.

### MC-NAT-04 — Marsh Heron · P1

**Imagine:** an original long-necked bird rising above reeds within a vertical panel. Wings and body are bold masses; reeds help support the legs without looking like accidental braces.

- **Targets:** proposed 400 × 800; matte black.
- **Construction:** feet, reeds, and lower frame join. Leg thickness is determined by the sample; avoid needle-like legs and sharp reed tips.
- **Buyer/display:** a sculptural nature piece for a hallway or reading corner.
- **Release condition:** silhouette reads as a bird, and the legs resist normal handling and packing.

### MC-NAT-05 — Honeycomb Flight · P1

**Imagine:** a cluster of open hexagonal cells with one stylized bee spanning several cells. Wings are broad cutout shapes, and a few cells remain solid for balance.

- **Targets:** proposed 600 × 500, later 800 × 670; matte black; muted bronze-color coating later.
- **Construction:** cells form a connected lattice; bee attaches to the lattice. Antennae and wing details are simplified, not hair-thin.
- **Buyer/display:** gardening and nature gifts; a compact decorative piece.
- **Release condition:** the smallest cells cut and finish cleanly, and the piece is clearly an original illustration rather than copied clipart.

### MC-NAT-06 — Climbing Vine screen · P2

**Imagine:** a repeating original vine pattern inside a tall rectangular metal border, used as a decorative garden panel or plant backdrop.

- **Target:** 400 × 1,000 initially, subject to bed size, stiffness, package, and transport constraints.
- **Construction:** robust border, generous openings, and a planned fixing system. Thickness and corrosion protection depend on intended exposure and supports.
- **Buyer/display:** garden walls and patios. No promise that it provides privacy, supports a particular plant load, or serves as a railing.
- **Release condition:** exterior material/coating and attachment validated; account for wind exposure and handling. No outdoor claim before that review.

## Original metal pictures

This is the strongest first interpretation of “painting carvings”: create our own composition specifically for retained metal and open space. A famous painting adaptation is a separate rights-reviewed project.

### MC-ART-01 — Orbit · P1

**Imagine:** overlapping arcs around a solid off-center disk, arranged within a circle. Open bands reveal the wall and cast a geometric shadow.

- **Targets:** proposed Ø650, later Ø850; matte black, warm white later. This is a large abstract wall composition.
- **Construction:** arcs join the disk or outer rim at intentional intersections. Avoid concentric rings with no supporting connections.
- **Buyer/display:** contemporary rooms; strong statement shape without text or an identifiable subject.
- **Release condition:** arcs retain their curve without twisting, and bridges feel like part of the composition.

### MC-ART-02 — Quiet Vase · P0

**Imagine:** a still-life picture of a broad vase, two leaves, a table edge, and a half-circle background. The original composition translates into a few solid shapes and open windows, like a graphic painting made from metal.

- **Targets:** proposed 500 × 700, later 650 × 910; matte black.
- **Construction:** create the composition from scratch for cutting. Attach vase, stems, table, and background to a shared frame. White areas in the drawing are holes, not white paint.
- **Buyer/display:** kitchens, dining areas, gifting; the most direct illustration of the metal-picture category.
- **Release condition:** viewers recognize the composition from a distance; nothing relies on shading, tiny stroke details, or unattached islands.

### MC-ART-03 — Tide Lines · P1

**Imagine:** three panels of flowing, broad wave bands. The visual rhythm continues across the gaps, with a calm crest in the middle panel.

- **Target:** proposed three 350 × 700 panels; 60 mm gaps create a 1,170 × 700 overall installation, excluding hardware projections.
- **Construction:** each panel is independently connected and mounted. Label left, center, and right; include an alignment guide in the eventual package.
- **Buyer/display:** above a sofa or desk; a higher-ticket set based on one coherent drawing.
- **Release condition:** sample the whole set at the intended gaps; pack each panel securely without metal rubbing metal.

### MC-ART-04 — Two Faces · P1

**Imagine:** two abstract profiles suggested by broad curved ribbons, with large open areas between them. This is an original fictional portrait, not a celebrity image or a trace of another artist's continuous-line print.

- **Targets:** proposed 550 × 750; matte black, warm white later.
- **Construction:** ribbons join a surrounding frame at planned points. Replace fine pen-like strokes with structural bands.
- **Buyer/display:** couples' gifts and expressive interior decor.
- **Release condition:** faces remain recognizable after structural simplification, and all contours form a connected object.

## Home objects and geometry

### MC-HOME-01 — Welcome Arch · P1

**Imagine:** an arched plaque with fixed “WELCOME” text and an original small leaf motif, presented as a ready design.

- **Target:** proposed 600 × 450; matte black; indoor use initially.
- **Construction:** our own stencil lettering or a font whose license permits the intended use and modification. Bridge enclosed letter centers, especially O. Mounting holes sit in the plate margin.
- **Buyer/display:** entrance walls; an easy-to-understand gift.
- **Release condition:** letters remain readable after cutting and finishing; the font/artwork provenance is recorded. A family-name version belongs to the personalized offer.

### MC-HOME-02 — Lattice Tile · P1

**Imagine:** one square tile with an original pattern of softened diamonds and broad intersecting bands. Four matching tiles create a larger wall arrangement.

- **Target:** proposed 500 × 500 per panel; sell individually or as a four-piece set after validation; matte black or warm white. These are substantial modular wall panels.
- **Construction:** pattern joins the outer rim. Mounting holes repeat consistently. Keep an outer margin so small installation offsets remain visually forgiving.
- **Buyer/display:** modular geometric decor for a hallway or home office.
- **Release condition:** test one tile and a four-tile arrangement. This is decorative wall art, not a ventilation cover, safety screen, or structural panel.

### MC-HOME-03 — Address Frame · P2 · personalized template

**Imagine:** a horizontal plaque with a large house number, a short street line, and a restrained original geometric border. The layout is premade; the text is unique to the order.

- **Target:** proposed 650 × 300; black coating, with exterior finish developed separately.
- **Construction:** stencil digits and text; enforce a validated text-length range and minimum letter features. Fixed holes belong to the border, not variable text.
- **Buyer/display:** house entrances; a bridge between ready designs and fully custom work.
- **Release condition:** customer approves the exact spelling and layout; outdoor protection and mounting validated. Do not promise illumination or reflective visibility.

### MC-HOME-04 — Branch Key Rail · P2

**Imagine:** a shallow wall rail with an original branch silhouette above four hooks. The decorative plate and functional hardware read as one object.

- **Target:** proposed 500 × 250 for the front plate; projection set by hook design. Functional development follows the large decorative assortment.
- **Construction:** either bent hooks from a validated design or separately attached hooks. A laser-cut flat outline alone is not a finished key rack.
- **Buyer/display:** entryways and functional housewarming gifts.
- **Release condition:** validate hook edges, coating, attachment, fasteners, mounting, and rated load for intended light items. No coat-rack claim without separate validation.

### MC-HOME-05 — Interlocking Bird · P2

**Imagine:** two broad original bird-shaped planes slot together into a small freestanding sculpture. One plane gives the body profile; the second adds wings and a stable footprint.

- **Target envelope:** proposed approximately 350 × 200 × 250 assembled; matte black. A substantial shelf sculpture rather than a miniature gift.
- **Construction:** no assumed universal slot width. Fit depends on actual sheet thickness, kerf, and coating build. Add retention if the parts can separate during normal handling.
- **Buyer/display:** desk or shelf sculpture, shipped assembled or flat with clear instructions after trials.
- **Release condition:** assembly fit, stability, exposed-edge finishing, and handling tested. Market as decor, not a toy.

### MC-HOME-06 — Horizon Bookends · P2

**Imagine:** two L-shaped bookends, each with a broad mountain silhouette cut into its upright section. The pair forms a landscape across a row of books.

- **Target:** proposed 200 × 250 upright and approximately 150 mm base depth per part; matte black with appropriate pads. A secondary functional concept, not the scale model for the main artwork collection.
- **Construction:** requires bending or joined parts, a suitable thickness, a stable base, and a finish that does not mark shelves or books.
- **Buyer/display:** functional office decor and book-lover gifts.
- **Release condition:** test sliding, tipping, shelf marking, and performance with representative books; confirm production repeatability after forming.

## Substantial occasion gifts and seasonal pieces

### MC-GIFT-01 — New Beginnings · P0

**Imagine:** a substantial stork carrying a bundle with a broad integrated congratulations/message area, inspired by the workshop's actual maternity gift. The bird, bundle, and lettering belong to one connected composition. Gift wrapping is part of the presentation direction.

- **Reference:** the [owner's photographed product](production-scale-reference.md) is **300 mm wide × 600 mm high**. Thickness is approximately **2–2.5 mm**, with exact gauge and alloy unconfirmed. Confirm the material, finish, and artwork source before defining the ready product specification.
- **Development baseline:** use the existing 300 × 600 mm envelope first. Other sizes require their own drawing and sample review; the earlier proposed 650 mm longest side is superseded by the owner's measurement.
- **Construction:** substantial body, broad wing/feather cutouts, connected bundle area, and reliable stencil lettering. “Честито!” is a possible ready message; use our own lettering or a permitted font. Names, dates, or a different message belong to a proofed personalized version.
- **Buyer/display:** gift for an expectant mother or a family welcoming a baby. Confirm the intended hanging/display arrangement rather than assuming it from the wrapped photo.
- **Release condition:** existing-production evidence is recorded; artwork rights and ready-message layout are checked; mounting and protective gift packaging are specified. A new mother-and-child silhouette is a follow-on original design, not automatically approved by this example.

### MC-SEAS-01 — Winter Forest · P1

**Imagine:** a substantial winter scene with a few bold spruce trees, a large geometric star, and a continuous lower landscape band. The whole composition is one connected decorative silhouette, suitable for seasonal display.

- **Targets:** proposed 650 × 500; black first, warm white later. There is no miniature hanging-ornament set in this assortment.
- **Construction:** broad connected tree masses, generous openings, supported star, and planned mounting points. Avoid lace-like snowflake perforation.
- **Buyer/display:** substantial seasonal gift or wall decoration; an original festive scene that can be displayed again each year.
- **Release condition:** validate the whole scene, display hardware, and large-piece packaging. Use original seasonal motifs rather than entertainment characters.

## Furniture development

### MC-FURN-01 — Cross Base side table · P2

**Imagine:** two broad slotted metal base planes crossing under a round top. The base has a restrained original arch cutout that echoes the wall-art collection. The finished object has a secure top connection, not simply loose sheets resting together.

- **Target envelope:** Ø450 top × 450 height, subject to structural design; matte black after the coating and joint process is proven.
- **Construction:** cut parts are only one stage. Determine thickness, locking joints, fasteners or welding, top support, floor pads, anti-racking behavior, edge treatment, and packaging through engineering and samples.
- **Buyer/display:** a future workshop statement piece, made to order at first if it reaches release readiness.
- **Release condition:** competent structural review and appropriate stability, load, durability, and joint tests for the intended market and use. No claimed load rating, furniture certification, or “easy assembly” promise before evidence exists.

### Beyond this catalog — chairs

Chairs are an exciting separate development path, not one of the 24 initial families. Start with an original bolted or welded frame concept and determine comfort, tipping, fatigue, joints, sharp-edge protection, floor contact, coating, and the applicable furniture requirements before deciding the sheet pattern. Producing a chair-shaped DXF is much less work than developing a chair people can safely use.

## How a concept becomes a product record

Keep the family ID stable while separating artwork revision from sellable variants. For example:

| Field | Example for the first zodiac prototype |
| --- | --- |
| Family | MC-ZOD-01 |
| Artwork design | Leo |
| CAD revision | Assigned when a reviewed drawing exists |
| Validated size | Actual approved width × height; 650 × 450 mm is the proposed Leo envelope, not a validated measurement |
| Material/thickness | Actual workshop selection, after sample validation |
| Finish | Actual matte-black coating process |
| Offer type | Ready design; stocked or made to order according to reality |
| SKU | Assigned only after this combination is approved |
| Includes | One substantial Leo silhouette; list actual supplied mounting parts |
| Rights evidence | Creator and original artwork record |

Do not use a concept ID as evidence of live stock. A new size may require geometry changes; a new finish may change slot fits; a new zodiac sign is a different drawing. Approve the actual combination being sold.
