## Two different limits to record

**Overall size** describes the complete object's width and height. **Feature size** describes its narrowest retained metal, smallest hole, closest cut spacing, smallest lettering detail, and exposed tips.

A large stork can still have an unworkable small letter or feather opening. A compact shape with broad solid areas may be easier than a much larger lace-like drawing. The latest owner clarification is that small detailed cuts are feasible, based on consultation with technical staff. Record numerical feature limits through operator checks and samples rather than carrying forward an assumed lower-size floor.

2. **Mother and child:** an original large silhouette with broad connected contours. A follow-on design possibility, not a proven product.

4. **Family tree:** a large branching composition with a small number of broad message areas, rather than miniature dangling name tags.
5. **New home:** a substantial house/branch silhouette with a ready message or bounded personalization.

These occasion themes can sit beside zodiac, automotive, landscape, and original metal pictures. They do not replace those interests; they show what “gift” means at this workshop's actual scale.

## Rules for the next revision of any design

- Start with an intended substantial silhouette or scene and its display/occasion use.
- Preserve the subject's natural outline where useful; do not force every design into a circular badge or small tile.
- Choose a proposed overall envelope, then check the smallest features independently.
- Prefer fewer readable cutouts and broad connected structure over miniature ornamentation.
- Design any message area for actual validated letters, including stencil bridges.
- Record the actual included mounting and gift packaging after samples; the photo alone does not prove the intended display method.
- Keep [rights review](rights-and-custom-boundaries.md) in place: a common stork/baby theme can be drawn independently, but an existing illustration is not automatically available to copy.
